Goal

Fix the late clock-in email alert so it uses the correct local time, correct attendance record, correct schedule time, and correct minutes late for that specific day only.

Do not break or affect anything else.

Do not change the existing attendance flow.
Do not change the existing clock-in or clock-out process.
Do not change how cleaners submit attendance.
Do not change schedule creation.
Do not delete or migrate existing attendance data unless approved.
Do not change unrelated notification templates.
Only fix the late clock-in alert logic, email data mapping, timezone handling, and View Attendance link behavior.

Issue 1: Email is showing the wrong clock-in time

Current problem

The app attendance record shows Fortune clocked in around 17:03 on April 27.

But the email shows:

Scheduled: 4/27/2026, 5:00:00 PM
Clocked In: 4/27/2026, 10:03:59 PM
Minutes Late: 304 min

This looks like a timezone conversion issue.

The clock-in time appears to be shifted by about 5 hours.

Required behavior

The email must show the same local time that appears in the attendance page.

Example:

If the attendance page shows:

Clocked in: 17:03

Then the email should show:

Clocked In: Apr 27, 2026 at 5:03 PM

Do not show UTC time in the email.
Do not show server time in the email.
Do not convert the same timestamp twice.
Do not use browser timezone for backend emails unless the company timezone is unavailable.

Use the company or business timezone as the source of truth.

For Clockfield, this should support local Canadian timezones properly, especially Winnipeg / Manitoba time.

Recommended timezone priority

Use this order:

Company/business timezone from company settings
Location/site timezone if location has its own timezone
User/admin timezone if company timezone does not exist
Final fallback: America/Winnipeg

Add a clear helper function if one does not already exist:

getCompanyTimezone(companyId)
formatInCompanyTimezone(date, companyTimezone)
getLocalBusinessDate(date, companyTimezone)

Use the same helper for:

scheduled time shown in email
clocked-in time shown in email
late minutes calculation
email send timing check
attendance lookup date

Issue 2: System thinks the next day starts around 7 PM

Current problem

Around 7 PM local time, the app behaves like it is already the next day.

This usually happens when the backend uses UTC dates directly.

Example:

7 PM in Winnipeg can already be the next UTC date depending on daylight saving/time offset.

Required behavior

Attendance and alerts must use the business local date, not UTC date.

When checking “today,” do not use:

new Date().toISOString().split('T')[0]

Do not use raw UTC date strings for attendance day matching.

Instead use:

localBusinessDate = getLocalBusinessDate(now, companyTimezone)

Example:

If company timezone is America/Winnipeg and local time is Apr 27 at 7:30 PM, the business date must still be Apr 27.

It should not become Apr 28 until midnight in the company timezone.

This fix should apply only to alert lookup and notification timing unless the same helper is already safely used elsewhere.

Issue 3: Minutes late must be calculated only for that specific shift

Current problem

The email appears to show total accumulated late minutes, like 304, 400, or 500 minutes.

That is confusing.

The late clock-in email should only show how late the cleaner was for that specific scheduled shift on that specific day.

Required behavior

Calculate minutes late like this:

scheduledStartLocal = schedule start time for this employee, location, and business date
clockInLocal = actual clock-in time for that attendance record

minutesLate = max(0, differenceInMinutes(clockInLocal, scheduledStartLocal))

Example:

Scheduled: Apr 27, 2026 at 5:00 PM
Clocked In: Apr 27, 2026 at 5:03 PM
Minutes Late: 3 min

Do not use:

monthly late total
weekly late total
employee total late minutes
attendance summary total
payroll late total
all-time late total

Only use the current attendance record and matching schedule.

Issue 4: Match the correct attendance record to the correct schedule

Required matching logic

When the late clock-in alert runs, it should match:

companyId
employeeId
locationId
businessLocalDate
scheduleId if available

Preferred match:

attendance.scheduleId === schedule.id

Fallback match:

same employee
same location
same business local date
clock-in timestamp within a reasonable window around scheduled shift

Do not pull a random schedule from the same day.
Do not compare UTC date to local attendance date.
Do not calculate lateness from the wrong shift.

Issue 6: Send alert only when the cleaner is late for that shift

Required behavior

Only send the alert when:

employee has a scheduled shift
employee clocked in after the allowed grace period
alert has not already been sent for this attendance or schedule event

Use a notification lock to avoid duplicate emails.

Recommended unique key:

late-clockin:{companyId}:{employeeId}:{scheduleId}:{businessLocalDate}

If scheduleId is not available:

late-clockin:{companyId}:{employeeId}:{locationId}:{businessLocalDate}:{clockInId}

Do not send repeated late alerts for the same clock-in.

Issue 7: View Attendance button must open the correct place

Current problem

The button should not just open a random page.

Required behavior

When admin clicks View Attendance:

If logged in:
Take them directly to the Clockfield attendance page.

If not logged in:
Take them to login first.
After successful login, redirect them to the attendance page.

Preferred URL format:

/login?redirect=/admin/attendance?employeeId=EMPLOYEE_ID&date=YYYY-MM-DD

Or, if your app already supports protected redirects:

/admin/attendance?employeeId=EMPLOYEE_ID&date=YYYY-MM-DD

The date in the URL must be the company local business date.

Example:

/admin/attendance?employeeId=fortune_id&date=2026-04-27

Make sure the attendance page can use the query params to preselect:

employee
date
location if available

Do not create a new attendance page.
Do not change the attendance page design unless needed to support the query params.
