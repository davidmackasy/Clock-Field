# Clockfield — Employee Fit for Duty Verification

## 1. Feature Name

**Fit for Duty Check**

This feature adds a mandatory pre-shift safety verification to the employee clock-in process.

Its purpose is to help employers confirm that employees are reporting to client locations fit, alert, unimpaired, and capable of safely performing their assigned work.

The verification must be completed before an employee can successfully clock in for their first shift.

---

# 2. Main User Flow

Current flow:

**Employee opens Clockfield → selects location/job → taps Clock In → employee is clocked in**

New flow:

**Employee opens Clockfield → selects location/job → taps Clock In → Fit for Duty Check appears → employee answers questions → employee accepts declaration → system evaluates answers → Clock In completes or is blocked/reviewed**

The Fit for Duty Check should normally appear only once for the employee's first clock-in for that shift.

If the employee clocks out temporarily and clocks back into the same active shift, the form should not automatically be required again unless:

* an administrator requests another verification;
* a configurable number of hours has passed;
* the employee starts a different scheduled shift;
* the employee starts work at another location where a new verification is required.

---

# 3. Employee Pop-Up

When the employee taps **Clock In**, do not immediately complete the clock-in.

Display a full-screen modal/page.

### Header

**Fit for Duty Check**

Supporting text:

> Before starting your shift, please confirm that you are fit and able to perform your duties safely today.

The employee must answer every required question before continuing.

---

# 4. Recommended Questions

Keep the questions short and focused on the employee's ability to work safely.

### Question 1

**Are you fit and able to safely perform your assigned duties today?**

Options:

**Yes**

**No**

Expected safe answer: **Yes**

---

### Question 2

**Are you currently under the influence of alcohol, cannabis, recreational drugs, or any other substance that could affect your ability to work safely?**

Options:

**Yes**

**No**

Expected safe answer: **No**

Do not ask the employee what specific substance they used unless an authorized supervisor later needs additional information as part of an incident investigation.

---

### Question 3

**Have you consumed anything before this shift that is currently affecting your judgment, coordination, concentration, reaction time, or ability to work safely?**

Options:

**Yes**

**No**

Expected safe answer: **No**

---

### Question 4

**Are you taking any medication or treatment that is currently affecting your ability to safely perform your assigned duties?**

Options:

**Yes**

**No**

Expected safe answer: **No**

Do not ask for the medication name or medical diagnosis through this routine form.

---

### Question 5

**Are you excessively tired, fatigued, or lacking enough alertness to safely perform your work?**

Options:

**Yes**

**No**

Expected safe answer: **No**

---

### Question 6

**Is there any illness, injury, physical limitation, or other condition today that prevents you from safely completing your assigned duties?**

Options:

**Yes**

**No**

Expected safe answer: **No**

The system should not request a diagnosis.

If the employee answers Yes, provide an option to contact the employer/supervisor to discuss whether modified duties or another arrangement is required.

---

### Question 7

**Do you believe you can work safely around customers, coworkers, equipment, chemicals, vehicles, and client property during this shift?**

Options:

**Yes**

**No**

Expected safe answer: **Yes**

---

### Question 8

**Is there anything affecting you today that you believe your supervisor should know about before you begin work?**

Options:

**No**

**Yes — I need to speak with my supervisor**

Avoid requesting sensitive details in a free-text box.

If Yes is selected, flag the employee for supervisor review.

---

# 5. Employee Declaration

After answering the questions, show a mandatory acknowledgement.

Checkbox:

☐ **I confirm that the answers I provided are true and accurate to the best of my knowledge. I confirm that I am fit to perform my assigned duties safely and understand that I must immediately report any change that could affect my ability to work safely.**

Underneath display:

### Workplace Fitness & Impairment Acknowledgement

> I understand that I must report to work and remain fit for duty throughout my shift. I must not perform work while impaired or otherwise unable to perform my duties safely.
>
> I understand that providing knowingly false information, reporting to work impaired, or continuing to work after becoming unfit for duty may result in removal from the worksite and disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, subject to company policy, applicable law, investigation, and any applicable accommodation requirements.
>
> I also understand that if a client, property owner, site manager, supervisor, or other authorized person raises a reasonable concern regarding my fitness for duty, my employer may investigate the matter and may require me to stop working while the situation is assessed.
>
> If I require workplace accommodation or support relating to a medical condition, disability, treatment, or substance dependency, I understand that I may contact my employer privately.

Employee cannot continue without checking:

**I have read and agree**

Button:

### Confirm & Clock In

---

# 6. Safe Clock-In Result

If all answers indicate the employee is fit for work:

1. Save the Fit for Duty record.
2. Save the acknowledgement.
3. Record the policy/declaration version.
4. Complete the employee's clock-in.
5. Show:

**You're Clocked In**

Secondary text:

**Fit for Duty Check completed successfully.**

---

# 7. Failed / Concerning Answer

Any unsafe answer should trigger a different workflow.

Examples:

* Not fit for work = HIGH RISK
* Currently impaired = HIGH RISK
* Substance currently affecting ability = HIGH RISK
* Medication currently impairing ability = REVIEW
* Excessive fatigue = REVIEW
* Condition preventing safe work = REVIEW
* Wants supervisor contact = REVIEW

For HIGH RISK answers:

### Do Not Complete Clock-In

Show:

**Clock-In Requires Supervisor Review**

> Based on your responses, we cannot automatically complete your clock-in. Please contact your supervisor before beginning work.

Buttons:

**Contact Supervisor**

**Close**

The employee must not be automatically marked as working.

Immediately notify administrators/supervisors.

# 14. Important Safety Rule

Never let an administrator silently change an employee's original answers.

Employee answers are immutable.

If an administrator clears an employee, store it separately:

**Original employee response**

*

**Supervisor decision**

*

**Supervisor name**

*

**Date/time**

*

**Optional supervisor note**

This preserves the audit trail.

# 20. Privacy & Permissions

These records contain sensitive employment information.

They should not be visible to everyone.

Recommended permissions:

Employee:

Can see own verification history.

Supervisor:

Can see employees assigned to locations they supervise.

Administrator:

Can view records for company employees.

Owner:

Full access.

Client:

**NO ACCESS**

Clients should never be able to open Clockfield and browse employee Fit for Duty questionnaires.

If a client reports suspected impairment, they can submit a concern, but the underlying employee questionnaire remains internal.

# 24. Critical Development Rules

Do not break the existing clock-in/clock-out system.

The Fit for Duty flow should intercept the clock-in request before the final clock-in database transaction.

A successful verification and clock-in should be connected using the same `shift_id` / `clock_in_id`.

Do not allow the frontend alone to decide whether someone can clock in.

The backend must verify:

1. employee is authenticated;
2. required questions were answered;
3. declaration was accepted;
4. responses pass company rules;
5. Fit for Duty record was saved;
6. only then create the clock-in.

This prevents someone from bypassing the questionnaire by directly calling the clock-in API.

# 25. Final Product Flow

**Employee**

Clock In

↓

**Fit for Duty Check**

↓

Answer questions

↓

Accept declaration

↓

### SAFE

Record submission → Clock employee in → Admin activity recorded

or

### FLAGGED

Record submission → Do not clock employee in → Alert supervisor → Supervisor reviews → Decision recorded.

The central principle is:

> Every employee's first clock-in should have a traceable Fit for Duty record showing what they declared, when they declared it, which policy they accepted, and whether Clockfield allowed them to begin the shift.
