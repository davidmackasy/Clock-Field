1. Main issue with current quote output

Right now the final Quote Output is locked too heavily to one display style.
It is showing Weekly amount, but that is not how many clients think about pricing.

Most clients want to see:
monthly amount
bi-monthly amount
quarterly amount
custom billing period

The quote needs to be customizable so the admin can choose which billing amount is shown on the final quote.

2. Add quote display period selector inside Quote Output

In the Quote Output section, add a compact dropdown called:

Display billing period

Options:
Per visit
Weekly
Bi-weekly
Monthly
Every 2 months
Quarterly
6 months
Custom

This dropdown controls what pricing format appears on the final public quote.

Important

This is a display and billing presentation selector for the quote output.
It should not break the internal suggestion engine.
It should use the generated pricing basis and convert it into the selected display period.

12. Pricing conversion rules for display period

Use the suggested pricing basis to calculate the displayed amount.

Examples

If per-visit price is $175
and service is 5 days/week

Monthly display:
$175 × 5 × 4 = monthly recurring subtotal

Quarterly display:
monthly subtotal × 3

6-month display:
monthly subtotal × 6

Important

Do not multiply one-time add-ons into each month unless the add-on is marked recurring.
One-time items should remain one-time line items.

Example

Recurring monthly cleaning:
$3,500/month

One-time startup services:
Deep clean: $300
Strip & wax: $2,000

Then the quote can show:
Monthly recurring service: $3,500
One-time startup services: $2,300
Subtotal: $5,800
Taxes
Total

This is more accurate than flattening everything.
