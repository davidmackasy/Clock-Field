PART 2: LINK EACH PHOTO TO WHAT THE USER SAID AROUND THAT PHOTO

Problem

Right now the photos and transcript are separate.
That is not the intended experience.

The user wants this behavior:
They speak while documenting the space.
They take a photo.
That photo should later show the note or transcript segment that matches what they were saying around that moment.

Example:
“This is the kitchen and the counters need deep cleaning.”
Then the picture is taken.
That kitchen image should later display that related transcript or AI note with it.

Required update

Each photo captured during a recording session must be linked to the transcript window around the time the photo was taken.

Required logic

When a photo is taken:
store the photo timestamp exactly

When transcript chunks are created:
store transcript start and end times exactly

Then assign each photo to the transcript chunk or transcript window nearest to that capture time.

Recommended correlation logic

For each image:
find the transcript chunk active at that timestamp
if none exists exactly, use the nearest chunk before and after within a small time window
merge nearby spoken context into one linked note if needed

Suggested time-link window:
primary match = transcript active at photo timestamp
fallback = nearest transcript within about 5 to 12 seconds before photo
secondary fallback = nearest transcript within about 3 to 8 seconds after photo

Do not use random matching.
Do not match one transcript block to all photos.
Do not keep them fully separate.

Expected output behavior in Session Detail

For every photo card or photo block:
show the image
show a short title generated from the related speech
show the related transcript snippet
show the AI-cleaned note for that image

Example visual structure

Photo 1
Area: Kitchen
Spoken note: “This is the kitchen and these counters need deep cleaning.”
AI note: Kitchen counters require deep cleaning and wipe-down. Check edges and backsplash area.

Photo 2
Area: Washroom
Spoken note: “This bathroom floor needs extra attention.”
AI note: Washroom floor requires detailed scrubbing and disinfecting.

This is one of the most important updates.
The pictures and speech must feel tied together.
