Employee should see a camera preview.
Allow Retake Photo before submitting.
Do not allow selecting an old image from the photo gallery.
The photo is part of the same Fit for Duty submission.
Do not use facial recognition or automatically determine whether the employee is drunk/high from their face.

Important Clock-In Rule

Once all questions are answered, a live photo has been captured, and the declaration is accepted, the employee must still be clocked in regardless of their questionnaire answers.

Do not introduce Supervisor Review Required or block clock-in.

If there are concerning questionnaire answers:

Save submission → Save live photo → Flag submission → Clock employee in normally → Notify admin.

Data
