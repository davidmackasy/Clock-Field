Current problem

The proposal currently shows a total like “Total $557.55,” but it does not clearly say whether that amount is monthly, yearly, per visit, or for the full contract. In the exported proposal, the service summary shows “Contract Length: 12 Months,” while the pricing section only shows “Total $557.55,” which could confuse clients. 

Required fix

The pricing section must clearly show the billing period.

Instead of only showing:

Total $557.55

It should show something like:

Monthly Total: $557.55 / month

or

Per Visit Total: $557.55 / visit

or

Annual Total: $6,690.60 / year

or

12-Month Contract Total: $6,690.60

The client must understand exactly what they are accepting.

4. Add Contract Length calculation

If the proposal has a contract length, calculate the full contract estimate.

Example:

Monthly Total: $557.55 / month
Contract Length: 12 months
Estimated 12-Month Total: $6,690.60

Formula:

contract total = billing period total × number of billing periods in contract

For Monthly + 12 months:

$557.55 × 12 = $6,690.60

For Weekly + 12 months:

weekly total × 52

For Bi-Weekly + 12 months:

bi-weekly total × 26

For Every 3 Months + 12 months:

quarterly total × 4

For Every 6 Months + 12 months:

6-month total × 2

For Yearly + 12 months:

yearly total × 1

If the contract length is custom or unclear, do not force calculation. Show only the billing period total.
