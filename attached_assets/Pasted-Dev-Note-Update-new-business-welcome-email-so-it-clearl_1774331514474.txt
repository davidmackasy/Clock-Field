Dev Note: Update new business welcome email so it clearly tells users they must subscribe before they can access Clockfield

Objective

Update the new business welcome email only.

The current welcome email says the business is set up and ready to go, but it does not clearly tell the user that they must complete a subscription before they can access the platform. This creates confusion because the user receives the email, tries to log in, and then gets blocked by the payment requirement.

We need to fix that by updating the welcome email content so it:

clearly tells the new business that the account has been created
clearly tells them they must complete a subscription before they can access the platform
includes a direct link/button to the subscription page
explains briefly what Clockfield will help them do after subscribing
ends with a clear call to action to complete subscription or open Clockfield
removes the support email line entirely

Critical scope lock

Only update the welcome email content and its CTA links for new business signups.

Do not touch:

signup logic
login logic

If anything outside this exact scope appears necessary, stop and ask for approval before proceeding.

Current issue:

new business signs up
receives welcome email
email suggests they are ready to use the platform
user clicks in and tries to log in
platform still requires subscription/payment before access
email did not prepare them for that next step

This causes confusion.

Required behavior after update

When a new business signs up:

they still receive the welcome email
the email must clearly say their business account was created
the email must clearly say they must complete subscription/payment before they can access the platform
the email must include a direct CTA button/link to the subscription page

The email should communicate this order clearly:

Once subscribed, you will be able to use Clockfield to manage your business
Open Clockfield / Complete subscription

Do not make the email sound like full access is already active if payment is still required.
