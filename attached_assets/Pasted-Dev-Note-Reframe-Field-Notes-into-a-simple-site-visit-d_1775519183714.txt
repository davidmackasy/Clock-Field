Dev Note: Reframe Field Notes into a simple site-visit document with optional quotation mode

You are right. The current version still sounds too AI-written and too formal in the wrong way. It reads like a generic compliance summary instead of a simple site-visit document the client can understand.

Also, the colored follow-up boxes should be removed. That is not the point of this page.

What this page should become

Field Notes should become one simple, professional walkthrough document.

Its main job is:

* show the photos taken during the site visit
* explain in plain language what was seen
* group the important things that need attention
* optionally turn the same walkthrough into a quotation/proposal

That matches how commercial cleaning bidding usually works: walkthrough details, measurements, photos, scope notes, then a professional bid or proposal built from that information. Good janitorial bid workflows emphasize taking measurements and pictures during the walkthrough, documenting room-specific details, and then turning that into a clear proposal with pricing and scope. Janitorial Manager also markets built-in bid calculation tied to facility details and ISSA-based standards, while Housecall Pro emphasizes polished estimates with line items, photos, signatures, and customer approval. ([Janitorial Manager][1])

New direction

Do not make Field Notes sound like:
“Several areas have been identified for updates to meet standards.”

Make it sound like a simple letter or walkthrough summary:
“These are the photos taken during the site visit. Based on the walkthrough, the main areas to focus on are the carpets, corner areas, and public-facing spaces.”

That is the tone.

Core content rules

Do not hard-rephrase what was spoken.
Do not inflate the language.
Do not create command-style follow-up boxes.
Do not add unnecessary “standards,” “compliance,” or “updates required” wording unless that was actually said or clearly intended.

Do this instead:

* keep the original meaning
* clean the grammar lightly
* turn rough speech into readable paragraphs
* keep the wording simple
* make it feel like a real note a company would send to a client

The document should read like:

* site visit summary
* what we saw
* what needs attention
* what may be included in service
* optional quote/proposal details if quotation mode is turned on

Field Notes should support two modes

1. Site Visit Report mode
   This is the plain walkthrough document.
   Used to show:

* what was seen
* what was photographed
* what areas need attention
* what the team is noting for internal review or client review

2. Quotation mode
   This uses the same walkthrough but adds pricing and scope structure.
   Used to show:

* service scope
* property details
* frequency
* pricing basis
* monthly / weekly / bi-weekly quote
* optional acceptance step

This should be a toggle on the admin side:

* Standard Field Note
* Turn into Quote / Proposal

Do not make quotation mode a separate unrelated feature. It should grow naturally from the walkthrough, because that is exactly what the walkthrough is for.

New document structure

For one site visit, render one document only.

Do not break it into fake mini-documents.

The new structure should be:

Document header

* Company name
* Client/site name
* Document type:

  * Site Visit Notes
  * Walkthrough Summary
  * Quote Proposal
* Visit date
* Prepared by
* Optional location

Opening paragraph
Simple and human.
Example structure:
“These are the notes and photos taken during the site visit on [date]. The purpose of this walkthrough was to review the space, document visible areas that need attention, and outline the main items to focus on.”

This section should not sound robotic.

Property summary section
This is where the walkthrough details live.
Fields should support:

* square footage
* number of rooms
* number of offices
* number of washrooms
* number of kitchens/break rooms
* number of hallways
* number of entrances
* number of floors
* upstairs/downstairs presence
* garage / warehouse / lobby / common areas
* special surfaces such as carpet, tile, VCT, glass, etc.

This is important because the user wants the walkthrough to become a quote later, and cleaning estimate tools usually depend on facility-specific details, room counts, and service scope to build accurate proposals. ([Janitorial Manager][2])

Main observations section

Do not use:

* Follow-up
* Recommendation box
* High/critical tags everywhere
* Too many colored labels

Instead, write clean paragraph sections.

Example structure:
Area observed
Plain paragraph explaining what was seen in that area.
If needed, a second short paragraph explaining what should be focused on.

Example tone:
“The carpeted areas appear to need regular attention. During the walkthrough, these sections were noted as areas that may require vacuuming more than once per week depending on traffic.”

Or:
“The corner areas shown in the photos were pointed out during the visit as spaces that need extra attention during service.”

Simple. Clear. Useful.

Photos section behavior

Do not dump all photos first and then disconnected notes after.

Instead, inside the document:

* show a short opening section
* then group photos with the matching paragraph or section
* if four photos belong to one area, keep them under that area
* if a photo has spoken context, keep that text with that photo group

The photos tab can still exist as a clean gallery.
But the document tab and public link must place photos in context.

Admin document tab redesign

The Document tab should feel like one readable document, not stacked AI cards.

Inside Document:

* one full document layout
* paragraph-based sections
* grouped photos
* property summary
* service-focus notes
* optional pricing section when quotation mode is on

Remove from the document experience:

* hard follow-up banners
* noisy observation chips
* excessive colored category tags
* multiple fake “documents” for one site visit

Transcript tab redesign

Transcript should stay available, but it should be clearly labeled as source transcript, not the final document.

Transcript tab should become:

* readable paragraphs
* ordered by capture sequence
* timestamps if available
* clean spacing
* optional photo references beside transcript chunk if matched

This helps the admin verify what was actually said without making the public link look messy.

Voice-to-data extraction for walkthrough

Add structured extraction from spoken walkthrough notes.

If the person says things like:

* “the square footage is 16 by 70”
* “there are 6 offices upstairs”
* “there is one kitchen in the back”
* “the carpets need vacuuming twice a week”

the system should try to detect and populate structured quote fields automatically.

That means the Field Note workflow should support extraction for:

* measurements
* square footage
* room counts
* service frequency
* floor type
* special tasks
* service notes

This should not replace manual editing.
The admin must still be able to review and adjust those fields before sending to client.

Quotation mode details

When quotation mode is turned on, add a quotation section below the walkthrough summary.

Suggested quotation block:

* Service frequency
* Scope summary
* Included areas
* Extra attention items
* Pricing basis
* Monthly price
* Weekly / bi-weekly option
* Add-ons
* Notes
* Acceptance / approval area

This matches what estimate software commonly does: present a professional estimate with line items, service descriptions, images, branding, and approval flow. ([Housecall Pro][3])

Quotation mode should also allow the admin to input or adjust:

* square footage
* number of rooms/offices
* service frequency
* carpet care frequency
* floor waxing frequency
* restroom service frequency
* trash service
* kitchen cleaning
* extra services
* monthly amount
* one-time deep clean amount
* notes for client

This should feel like a production quoting tool, not an AI summary page.

Public link redesign

The public link must become client-ready.

It should look like:

* a professional walkthrough summary
* or a quote proposal if quote mode is on

Public link should be simple:

* title
* company
* site/client
* date
* prepared by
* short summary paragraph
* grouped photo sections with plain-language notes
* optional service scope section
* optional pricing section
* optional acceptance area

The public link should read like a clean document somebody can open and understand in one pass.

Tone rules for the generated writing

Use language like:

* “These are the photos taken during the walkthrough.”
* “The main areas to focus on are…”
* “Based on the site visit, attention should be given to…”
* “The carpets, hallways, and corner areas were noted during the visit.”
* “This quote is based on the information gathered during the walkthrough.”

Avoid language like:

* “meet standards”
* “compliance risk”
* “critical update required”
* “follow-up action”
  unless those ideas were actually stated and truly necessary.

The result should sound like a business letter, not an AI warning system.
