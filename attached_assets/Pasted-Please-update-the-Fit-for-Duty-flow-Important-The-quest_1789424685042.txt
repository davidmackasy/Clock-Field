Please update the Fit for Duty flow.

**Important:** The questionnaire must NOT block the employee from clocking in, regardless of how they answer.

Right now, the system can show:

**“Supervisor Review Required — Based on your responses, your clock-in cannot be completed automatically. Please contact your supervisor before beginning work.”**

Remove this blocking behavior completely.

The new required flow is:

**Employee enters Employee ID + Password → Fit for Duty questionnaire appears → Employee answers all questions → Employee confirms declaration → Employee is clocked in normally.**

This must happen even if the employee answers:

* No, they are not fit to work
* Yes, they are affected by alcohol
* Yes, they are affected by drugs or another substance
* Yes, something is affecting judgment or coordination
* Yes, they are too fatigued
* Yes, there is another reason they may not be able to work safely

The employee should still be clocked in.

### What happens with concerning answers

Instead of blocking clock-in:

1. Save the complete questionnaire submission.
2. Complete the employee's normal clock-in.
3. Mark the questionnaire internally as **Flagged** if any concerning answer is selected.
4. Immediately send the submission/alert to the admin.
5. Let the admin review the employee's answers from the Fit for Duty section.

Example admin alert:

**Fit for Duty Alert**

**Employee:** John Doe
**Location:** Store A
**Clock-in:** 8:02 PM
**Status:** Flagged

**Reason:** Employee provided one or more responses that may require review.

Admin should be able to open the notification and see all original answers.

### Employee-side behavior

After they submit the questionnaire, simply show:

**Clock-In Successful**

“Your Fit for Duty Check has been submitted.”

Then continue into the existing employee account exactly as the normal clock-in currently does.

Do NOT show:

* Supervisor Review Required
* Clock-in blocked
* Contact your supervisor before beginning work
* Waiting for approval
* Pending supervisor review

Do NOT require an admin to approve the employee before clock-in.

### Backend behavior

The Fit for Duty questionnaire is now an **information and alerting system**, not a clock-in authorization system.

The backend flow should be:

**Save questionnaire → determine Cleared/Flagged status → create existing clock-in → notify admin if flagged.**

The questionnaire submission should remain attached to:

* employee_id
* location_id
* shift_id
* clock_in_id
* submitted_at
* all questions and answers
* declaration accepted
* status: Cleared or Flagged

### Critical

Do not change anything else in the existing employee clock-in system.

Do not redesign authentication.

Do not alter clock-out.

Do not alter shifts.

Do not alter locations.

Do not add supervisor approval before clock-in.

We only want:

**Existing Login → Questionnaire → Always Clock In → Save Submission → Notify Admin if Needed.**

Please update the current implementation to follow this exact behavior.
