Please add admin email notifications for Fit for Duty submissions.

Keep the current in-app Fit for Duty admin page exactly as it is. Do not remove or replace the existing dashboard notification. Email should be an additional notification only.

Required behavior

Every time an employee submits the Fit for Duty questionnaire:

Save submission → Clock employee in normally → Show submission in Admin Fit for Duty → Send email notification to authorized admin email address(es).

This applies to both:

Cleared submissions
Flagged submissions

Flagged submissions should have a more urgent subject line.

Example email — Cleared

Subject: Fit for Duty Submission — David Maluti — Cleared

Employee: David Maluti
Employee ID: EMP-1005
Date/Time: September 14, 2026, 3:35 PM
Location/Shift: [existing location or shift]
Status: Cleared
Confirmation: Accepted

Questionnaire responses:

Fit and able to safely perform duties — Yes
Currently affected by alcohol/drugs/substances — No
Anything affecting judgment/coordination — No
Excessively tired/fatigued — No
Any other reason unable to work safely — No

View submission in ClockField

Example email — Flagged

Subject: ⚠ Fit for Duty Alert — David Maluti — Flagged

Employee: David Maluti
Employee ID: EMP-1005
Date/Time: September 14, 2026, 3:35 PM
Status: Flagged

Attention: One or more responses may require admin review.

Include all questionnaire answers in the email and clearly highlight the answers that caused the submission to be flagged.

Add a button/link:

View Full Submission

This should open the related Fit for Duty record inside the existing admin panel.

Live Photo

Since the submission now includes a live face photo, do not attach the actual photo directly to the email by default.

The email should say:

Live Photo: Captured

Admin can click View Full Submission to securely see the photo inside ClockField.

This is better for privacy and avoids placing employee photos in email inboxes.

Admin recipients

Send notifications only to authorized company admins.

Use the company/admin email records that already exist in ClockField. Do not hard-code one email address.

If there are multiple authorized admins, support sending to all admins who have Fit for Duty email notifications enabled.

Recommended setting

Add a simple setting:

Settings → Notifications → Fit for Duty Emails

Email me all submissions
Email me flagged submissions only
Off

Default can be:

Flagged submissions only

unless the company enables all submissions.

Important

Email sending must not delay or prevent the employee from clocking in.

The employee flow must remain:

Submit questionnaire → Save live photo → Clock in successfully

Email should be processed separately after the submission is saved.

If email delivery fails, the employee must still remain clocked in and the Fit for Duty submission must still appear in the admin dashboard.

Do not change any existing authentication, clock-in, clock-out, Fit for Duty questions, live photo, or admin review functionality.
