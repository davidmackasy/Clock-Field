19. DIRECT BUILD INSTRUCTION FOR AGENT

Add a new cleaner-side Work Capture workflow using a centered plus button in the mobile bottom navigation. This feature must remain separate from clock-in/out and attendance. After the cleaner clocks in, they can tap the plus button to start a work submission, confirm or select their scheduled location, then add multiple categorized work items such as washrooms, offices, floors, kitchen, stairs, and other areas. Each work item should support a section, a sub-area label, optional notes, before photos, and after photos. The cleaner can continue adding multiple work items and then submit the entire batch as one work submission. On the admin side, add a new Work Log page where submissions are listed by employee, date, and location, and where admin can open a submission to see all categorized work items and their photo evidence. Keep this fully additive and do not modify any working clock-in, attendance, payroll, schedule, request, or auth functionality.
