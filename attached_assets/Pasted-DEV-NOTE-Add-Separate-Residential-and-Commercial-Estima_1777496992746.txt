DEV NOTE: Add Separate Residential and Commercial Estimator Settings
Goal:
Update Estimator Settings so residential and commercial quotes use separate pricing rules.
Do not break or affect anything else.
Only update Forms / Quote Requests → Estimator Settings and AI Quote Estimator logic.


Current issue


Estimator Settings currently uses one shared pricing setup.
This is not accurate because:
Residential pricing is different from commercial pricing.
Commercial jobs often need different minimums, square footage rates, crew size, frequency logic, and multipliers.


Required update


Create two estimator pricing profiles:
Residential Pricing
Commercial Pricing
Add tabs inside Estimator Settings:
Residential
Commercial
Each tab should have its own saved values.

Estimate logic


When form is submitted, check:
property_type = residential
or
property_type = commercial
If residential:
Use Residential Pricing settings only.
If commercial:
Use Commercial Pricing settings only.
Do not mix pricing rules.


AI Quote Estimator prompt/data


Pass this clearly to the AI:
property_type
selected_pricing_profile
pricing_settings_for_selected_type
frequency details
form answers
uploaded photos
service type
square footage
rooms / bathrooms / washrooms
notes
Important:
If property_type is commercial, do not send residential bedroom/bathroom pricing unless it was actually collected.
If property_type is residential, do not send commercial office/washroom pricing unless it was actually collected.


Submission result


The generated estimate must show:
Quote type:
Residential or Commercial
Pricing profile used:
Residential Pricing or Commercial Pricing
Then show:
Per visit estimate
Monthly estimate if recurring
Contract total if contract length exists
Labor hours
Crew size
Scope of work


Fallback behavior


If the correct pricing profile is missing:
Show warning in admin:
“Commercial pricing settings are incomplete. Estimate used fallback defaults.”
Do not crash.
Do not block form submission.
