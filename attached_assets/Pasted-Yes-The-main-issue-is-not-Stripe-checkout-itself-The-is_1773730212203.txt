Main goals

If a business successfully subscribes through Stripe, its subscription must become active right away after confirmation.

The business must keep access to the platform according to its subscribed plan.

If the business clicks a feature not included in its plan, do not sign them out and do not send them to the full paywall page.

Instead, show an in-app upgrade prompt/modal explaining that the feature requires a higher plan.

If they cancel the prompt, they stay inside their account and keep using the features included in their current plan.
