DEV NOTE: Fix Schedule, Attendance, Clock-In Time Zone Matching Bug

Issue:
Attendance is matching employee clock-ins to the wrong schedule template.

Example:
Today is Sunday.
Employee Fortune Eiwo has a Sunday schedule from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM.
Employee clocked in around 7:56 AM.
Attendance incorrectly matched this clock-in to the weekday schedule from 5:00 PM to 10:30 PM.
The system then shows a false variance of 3,776+ minutes late.

This is wrong.

The employee should be matched to the Sunday schedule because the clock-in happened on Sunday local business time.

Important instruction:
Do not change, delete, rebuild, or refactor the schedule system.
Do not change the clock-in flow.
Do not change attendance records.
Do not change payroll.
Do not change recurring schedules.
Do not touch anything outside this bug fix.

Only fix the date, time zone, and schedule-matching logic.

Main fix required:

Use the business/account local timezone everywhere
The app must use the company timezone from account settings.

For Clockfield, this should likely be:
America/Winnipeg

All schedule matching must be based on the business local date, not UTC date.

Do not use raw server UTC day when deciding which schedule applies.

Match clock-ins by local calendar day
When an employee clocks in:

Convert the clock-in timestamp to the business timezone.
Get the local date.
Get the local weekday.
Find the employee schedule assigned to that exact weekday.

Example:
Clock-in timestamp converted to America/Winnipeg = Sunday, May 3, 2026, 7:56 AM.
The system must match Sunday schedules only.

It must not match Monday, weekday, or previous/next day schedules because of UTC conversion.

Fix recurring schedule lookup
Recurring schedules must be selected by:

employee_id
business_id
local weekday
active schedule status
date range validity
schedule start/end date
not paused or deleted

If employee has multiple schedules, choose the one where the recurring day matches the local clock-in day.

Fix attendance variance calculation
Variance must compare:

actual clock-in local time
against
matched schedule start local time

Example:
Sunday schedule: 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM
Actual clock-in: 7:56 AM
Result:
4 minutes early

It should not show late.
It should not show thousands of minutes late.

Protect overnight shifts
Some schedules may cross midnight, like 5:00 PM to 10:30 PM or overnight shifts.

Do not break overnight logic.

Rules:
If schedule ends after start time, treat it as same-day.
If schedule end time is earlier than start time, treat it as overnight and ends the next local day.
Only use overnight matching when the schedule actually crosses midnight.

Do not accidentally pull a Monday schedule into Sunday unless the shift started Sunday and ended Monday.

Fix forgotten clock-out banner
The forgotten clock-out alert must use the matched schedule’s correct local end time.

If the employee is on the Sunday 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM schedule, the alert must use Sunday 2:00 PM.
It must not show weekday 10:30 PM.

Add safe validation logs
Add temporary server-side debug logs only for this matching logic.

Log:
employee id
business timezone
raw clock-in timestamp
local clock-in date
local weekday
matched schedule id
matched schedule day
matched schedule start/end
variance result

Do not expose logs to users.

Add test cases
Add tests for:

Late clock-in shows correct late minutes
