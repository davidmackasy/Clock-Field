Critical instruction:
Do not break or affect anything else in ClockField.

Current problems

The Requests tab is wrong right now.

Issues shown:

1. Admin cannot add supply to a location from this page.
2. Admin cannot assign supply to a cleaner/employee location properly.
3. The old “Add Supply” concept was removed.
4. Buttons like Ordered, Resolve, and Not Needed were added, but they do not match the workflow.
5. Fulfill from Inventory is returning this error:

400: {"message":"locationId and quantity required"}

6. The Fulfill modal does not include Location.
7. The Fulfill modal does not include Cleaner/Employee.
8. The page is treating an admin-added supply like a cleaner request.
9. Request actions are causing 404 or confusing status changes.
10. The cleaner cannot properly receive assigned supplies for their location.

Correct concept

This tab must support two separate things:

1. Admin Added Supplies
   Admin adds supply from inventory to a location and assigns it to the cleaner/employee at that location.

2. Cleaner Requests
   Cleaner reports that an existing assigned supply is out, low, missing, or needs replacement.
   Cleaner can also request a brand-new supply that is not already listed.

The admin page needs to show both, but they must not be mixed together.

Data rules

Inventory is company stock.

Location Supplies are items assigned to a location from inventory.

Cleaner Requests are messages/reports from employees.

Location Expenses are cost records created when inventory is assigned or restocked to a location.

Purchase History is what company bought.

Do not mix these records.

Inventory deduction rule

When assigning or restocking:

new_inventory_quantity = current_inventory_quantity - assigned_quantity

Never allow negative inventory.

If current stock is 1 and admin enters 2:

Show:
Only 1 unit is available in inventory.

Do not submit.

Location expense rule

total_expense = quantity × unit_price_at_time

Example:
1 Pinky × $29 = $29

This should appear under Location Expenses.

Use unit_price_at_time.
Do not change old expense records if inventory price changes later.
