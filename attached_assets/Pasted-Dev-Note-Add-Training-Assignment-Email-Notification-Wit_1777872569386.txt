Dev Note: Add Training Assignment Email Notification Without Breaking Existing Training Flow

Goal

Add an email notification option when a training course is assigned to employees/cleaners.

When an employee is assigned to a training, they should receive a clear email telling them:

They have been assigned a training

The training title/topic

That it is required or optional, depending on assignment settings

Where to find it inside the app

How to complete it

That they can complete it anytime before the due date, if a due date exists

Important: Do not change, delete, rebuild, or affect the current training/course creation system. This is only an email notification upgrade for assigned employees.

Core Requirement

When a course/training is assigned to one or more employees, the system should support email notifications using the existing Clockfield email system.

Do not add a new third-party email provider.

Do not replace the current email setup.

Do not touch unrelated Mailgun, auth, Stripe, attendance, clock-in, schedules, payroll, reports, or worklog logic.

Use the existing email service/helper already used in the app.

Feature Behavior

When assigning a training to employees

Add a simple email notification option in the assignment flow.

Example checkbox:

Send email notification to assigned employees

Default behavior:

Checked by default if safe

Or unchecked if we want admin control

Do not force-send emails without giving the admin visibility.

When the admin assigns employees and saves the assignment, the selected employees should receive the training assignment email.

Send email to all assigned employees

If multiple employees are selected, send the email to each assigned employee.

Each employee should receive their own email.

Do not expose other employees’ emails.

Do not use CC with all employees.

Use individual email sending or BCC only if the existing system already safely supports it.

Best option: one email per employee.

Avoid duplicate emails

Do not send a duplicate email every time the admin opens or edits the course.

Only send email when:

A new employee is assigned to a course

Or admin manually clicks Send reminder email

If an employee was already assigned before, do not send another assignment email unless the admin chooses to resend.

Optional resend button

Inside the course assignment/admin view, add a small action:

Send reminder email

This should let the admin resend the training notification to employees who have not completed the training yet.

Do not send reminders to employees who already completed the training unless the admin specifically selects them.

Email Content

Subject example:

New Training Assigned: [Training Title]

Email body example:

Hi [Employee Name],

You have been assigned a training course in Clockfield.

Training: [Training Title]

Assigned by: [Company Name]

Status: Required

Due Date: [Due Date if available]

Please complete this training when you get a chance.

How to complete it:

Log in to your Clockfield employee app.

Go to your Profile.

Open the Training section.

Select the assigned training.

Review the course and complete the quiz or required steps.

Click below to open Clockfield:

[Open Training]

Thank you,
[Company Name]

Email Link Behavior

The email should include a button:

Open Training

Preferred behavior:

If possible, link directly to the employee training page.

Example:

/employee/profile/training

or the existing route used for the employee training tab.

If direct linking is not safe or the route requires auth, still send them to the app login/dashboard.

After login, they should be able to go to:

Profile → Training

Important:

Do not create a public training link unless the current training system already supports it.

This email is only for assigned employees inside the app.

Keep the training access protected behind employee login.

UI Changes

Admin side

In the training assignment modal/page, add:

Checkbox:

Send email notification to assigned employees

Optional helper text:

Employees will receive an email with instructions to open the app, go to Profile, and complete the assigned training.

After assigning, show a clean success toast:

Training assigned successfully. Email notification sent to assigned employees.

If emails fail but assignment succeeds:

Training assigned successfully, but one or more email notifications could not be sent.

Do not fail the assignment just because email failed.

Employee side

No major UI changes required for this upgrade.

Keep the existing employee training page and reminder UI working as-is.

Do not change the course viewer, quiz flow, completion tracking, or homepage reminder unless needed only to support email links.

Data Handling

Add fields only if needed.

Recommended assignment/email tracking fields:

emailNotificationSentAt

lastReminderEmailSentAt

emailNotificationStatus

emailNotificationError

These can be stored on the training assignment record if the existing schema supports it.

Do not migrate or rewrite unrelated training data.

Do not delete current assignments.

Do not change current completion logic.

Backend Logic

Create or reuse a service function like:

sendTrainingAssignmentEmail({
employee,
course,
company,
assignment,
})

The function should:

Check employee has a valid email

Use existing email system

Build a clean branded email

Include course title

Include company name

Include due date if available

Include login/app link

Handle errors safely

Log failed email sends without stopping the training assignment

Email sending should happen after the assignment is successfully saved.

Do not send email before the database assignment is created.

Manual Reminder Logic

Add an admin action:

Send reminder email to incomplete employees

This should:

Find assigned employees for the course

Filter employees where completion status is not completed

Send reminder email

Update lastReminderEmailSentAt

Show success/failure toast

Reminder subject example:

Reminder: Complete Your Assigned Training

Reminder body example:

Hi [Employee Name],

This is a reminder that you still have an assigned training to complete in Clockfield.

Training: [Training Title]

Please log in to your employee app, go to Profile → Training, and complete it when you can.

[Open Training]

Thank you,
[Company Name]

Important Safety Rules

Do not touch or rebuild:

Course creation

Module creation

Quiz creation

Training completion logic

Employee training viewer

Homepage reminder popup

Employee profile page, except if needed to support the training link

Auth

Stripe

Payroll

Attendance

Schedules

Worklogs

Reports

Field notes

Public links

Only add notification logic around training assignments.

Email System Requirement

Use the current app email system only.

Do not introduce a new email provider.

Do not add a new dependency unless absolutely required.

Do not create a separate email configuration.

Use the existing company branding/email template if available.

If the app already supports company logo/name in emails, reuse it.

If no branded email template exists, create a simple internal training email template using the current email sender.

Failure Handling

If email fails:

Training assignment should still be saved.

Admin should see a clear message.

Log the error for debugging.

Do not crash the page.

Do not rollback the assignment unless assignment itself fails.

Testing Checklist

Test assigning one employee to a training.

Confirm the employee receives one email.

Confirm the email includes the correct training title.

Confirm the email includes the correct company name.

Confirm the email tells the employee to go to Profile → Training.

Confirm the email button opens the app.

Test assigning multiple employees.

Confirm each employee gets their own email.

Confirm no employee sees another employee’s email.

Test assigning an employee who already had the course.

Confirm no duplicate assignment email is sent unless manually resent.

Test the Send reminder email button.

Confirm reminders only go to incomplete employees.

Complete the training as employee.

Confirm the reminder no longer sends to that employee.

Test email failure.

Confirm assignment still works.

Confirm the admin sees a clean message.

Expected Result

Admins can assign training like they already do.

They can choose to notify assigned employees by email.

Employees receive a clear email explaining what training they need to complete and where to go.

The employee can open Clockfield, go to Profile → Training, and complete the course.

Nothing else in the app is changed, broken, removed, or rebuilt.
