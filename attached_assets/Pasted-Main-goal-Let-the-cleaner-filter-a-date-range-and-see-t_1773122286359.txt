Do not overcomplicate it with payroll.
Do not calculate from schedule only.
Use real worked time only.

Custom Range

Start Date

End Date

When the cleaner selects a range:

show total worked hours for that range

show all shifts worked in that range

each row should show date, clock-in, clock-out, total hours

keep the current flags if they already exist

Main summary card
Show:

Total Hours Worked

Total Shifts Worked

Important logic
Use only real time-entry data:

clock_in_at

clock_out_at

worked_minutes

If the cleaner was scheduled but did not work, do not count that time.
