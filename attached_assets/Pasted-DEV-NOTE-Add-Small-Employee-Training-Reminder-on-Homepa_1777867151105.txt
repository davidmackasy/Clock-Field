DEV NOTE: Add Small Employee Training Reminder on Homepage
Critical rules

Do not change course creation.

Do not change quiz creation.

Do not change module editing.

Do not change training assignment logic.

Do not change certificates.

Do not change clock-in or clock-out behavior.

Only add a small reminder on the employee homepage when required training is incomplete.

Goal

When an employee is assigned a required training course, show a small reminder card on their homepage.

The reminder should appear only when the employee has training that is:

Assigned
Required
Not completed
Failed and needs retake
In progress but not finished

Once the employee completes and passes the required training, the reminder should disappear.

Required homepage reminder

Add a small card near the top of the employee homepage.

Recommended title:

Required Training

Recommended message examples:

If not started:

You have required training to complete: Cleaning Safety Course.

If in progress:

You are 60% done with Cleaning Safety Course. Please finish your required training.

If failed and retake is needed:

You need to retake Cleaning Safety Course to complete your required training.

Button:

Continue Training

Button action:

Take employee directly to the assigned training page.

Multiple assigned trainings

If the employee has more than one incomplete required training:

Only show one card.

Do not show multiple cards.

Priority order:

Failed training that requires retake
In-progress training with highest completion percentage
Not-started required training
Oldest assigned required training

Add small text:

1 of 3 required trainings

Example:

Required Training
You are 60% done with Cleaning Safety Course. Please finish your required training.
1 of 3 required trainings
Continue Training

Do not crowd the homepage.

Visibility rules

Show reminder when:

Employee has at least one required assigned training not completed
Training is in progress
Training was failed and retake is required

Hide reminder when:

Employee has no required assigned training
Employee completed and passed all required training
Training assignment was removed
Course is unpublished or unavailable
Progress logic

Use existing training progress data.

Do not create duplicate progress records.

Reminder percentage should use existing completion calculation:

Completed modules / total modules

If quiz is required, include quiz completion/pass status in final completion.

Examples:

5 modules total
3 completed
Progress = 60%

If modules are complete but quiz is failed:

Show retake message, not 100% complete.

UI requirements

Card style:

Small and clean
Not a large popup
Should not block clock-in / clock-out
Should not feel annoying
Should fit mobile and desktop

Suggested layout:

Title: Required Training
Body: Short progress message
Badge: Required
Progress bar: optional but recommended
Button: Continue Training

Use app’s existing card style.

Do not introduce a new design system.

Suggested UI copy

Not started:

Required Training
You have required training to complete: [Course Title].
Continue Training

In progress:

Required Training
You are [progress]% done with [Course Title]. Please finish your required training.
Continue Training

Failed / retake:

Required Training
You need to retake [Course Title] to complete your required training.
Retake Training

Multiple trainings:

Required Training
You are [progress]% done with [Course Title].
1 of [total] required trainings
Continue Training

Click behavior

When employee clicks the card or button:

Navigate to the training course page
Open the correct assigned course
Continue from their current progress if supported
Do not restart completed modules unless retake is required
Data connection

Use existing assigned training data.

Fetch only trainings assigned to the logged-in employee.

Filter:

required = true
completed = false OR passed = false OR retakeRequired = true

Sort by priority:

retakeRequired / failed
in progress
not started
assignedAt ascending

Return only the top item for homepage display.

Also return total incomplete required count.

Backend/API behavior

Add or reuse endpoint:

GET /api/employee/training/reminder

Expected response:

{
  hasReminder: true,
  totalRequiredIncomplete: 3,
  training: {
    assignmentId: "assignment_id",
    courseId: "course_id",
    title: "Cleaning Safety Course",
    status: "in_progress",
    progressPercent: 60,
    required: true,
    retakeRequired: false
  }
}

If no reminder:

{
  hasReminder: false,
  totalRequiredIncomplete: 0,
  training: null
}

Use current auth/session employee ID.

Do not expose other employees’ training.

Frontend placement

Employee app homepage only.

Place near the top, but below any critical clock-in status card if needed.

Do not place inside admin homepage.

Do not place on client/public pages.

Completion behavior

After employee completes and submits training:

Recalculate assignment status
If passed/completed, remove reminder from homepage
If failed and retake is required, show retake reminder
If there are other incomplete required trainings, show the next one
Edge cases

If course was deleted:

Do not show reminder.

If course is unpublished:

Do not show reminder.

If training has no modules:

Do not show reminder unless course is valid.

If progress is missing:

Treat as not started.

If employee has completed modules but failed quiz:

Show retake message.

If employee has 3 required trainings:

Show only one card and count text.

Testing required

Test 2:

Test 3:

Homepage shows retake message.

Test 6:
Assign 3 required courses.

Homepage shows only one card.

Text shows 1 of 3 required trainings.

Test 7:
Remove assignment.

Reminder disappears.

Test 8:
Unpublish course.

Reminder disappears.

Test 9:
Confirm clock-in and clock-out still work unchanged.

Expected result

Employees see a clean reminder only when they have required training to finish.

The reminder is small, useful, and connected to real training progress.

Once training is completed, it disappears automatically.

No course creation, quiz, module, certificate, assignment, or clocking system is changed.
