Yes. This is a storage persistence issue, not just an image rendering issue.

What is happening
Your uploaded images are likely being stored in a local filesystem path like /uploads/... inside the app container or server instance. When you redeploy or republish, that environment gets rebuilt, and those local files can disappear or their paths stop being valid.

That is why:

images looked fine before deploy

after republish, older submitted images are broken

For a production app, uploaded files must not rely on temporary deploy storage.
